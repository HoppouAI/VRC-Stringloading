DIG-N-BUILD

PACK MAKER

Change the sounds, the music or the textures in the Dig-n-Build world. Make the pack here, put it online, then paste the link on the PACKS page in the world. Only you see and hear your pack.

Name your files after the sound they replace, like step_grass1.wav, and they go in the right spot when you add them or drop them here. Every sound you leave alone keeps the normal Dig-n-Build sound. To start from the real ones, get the normal sounds as WAV files, edit the ones you want and drop them back in.

    QUESTMAX

    A texture pack is one 2048 by 2048 PNG image. Download a sheet, paint over it, keep everything where it is, and save it at the same size. Leave the empty spots empty.

    DIG-N-BUILD SHEET CLASSIC SHEET
    • BLOCKS The textures on the blocks in the world, 16 by 16 tiles.
    • BLOCK ICONS The block pictures in your inventory, hotbar, chests and furnaces.
    • ITEMS Item icons. Tools, food, doors and the bow you hold use these too.
    • MOBS Mob skins, arrows, the fishing bobber and the minecart.
    • MENUS Buttons, panels, hearts, hunger and the hotbar.

    How it works

    1. Pick SOUNDS, MUSIC or TEXTURES and add your files.
    2. Press BUILD PACK to save your pack file. A texture pack is just the image you painted.
    3. Put the file online. See below.
    4. In the world, open the options menu and press the PACKS button. Pick the same tab, paste your link and press LOAD.
    • Only you see and hear your pack. Everyone else keeps their own.
    • Paste your link again each time you join.
    • Quest only loads sound and music packs up to 64 MB. The bar under your pack shows where that is.

    Putting it online

    GitHub Pages works for everyone. Other sites only load for players who turned on Allow Untrusted URLs in their VRChat settings.

    1. Make a GitHub account and a new public repository.
    2. Upload your pack file to it. The website only takes files up to 25 MB, so use GitHub Desktop for bigger ones.
    3. In the repository, open Settings, then Pages, and have it deploy from the main branch.
    4. After a minute your link works. It looks like this:https://yourname.github.io/yourrepo/my-sounds.bin

    Texture packs can also go on Imgur. Use the direct i.imgur.com link to the image.

    Your files never leave your computer. Packs are built right here in your browser.
