        SHEET TOOLS

        A texture pack is one 2048 by 2048 PNG image. Start from a template, paint over it, keep everything where it is and save it at the same size. Point at the sheet below to see what each spot is for.

        CLASSIC TEMPLATE DIG-N-BUILD TEMPLATE

        • BLOCKS The textures on the blocks in the world. 16 by 16 tiles, each with an 8 pixel border that repeats the edge.
        • BLOCK ICONS The block pictures in your inventory, hotbar, chests and furnaces.
        • ITEMS Item icons. Tools, food, doors and the bow you hold use these too.
        • MOBS Mob skins, arrows, the fishing bobber and the minecart.
        • MENUS Buttons, panels, hearts, hunger, the hotbar and the burning overlay.

        SOUNDS

        Name your files after the sound they replace, like step_grass1.wav, and they go in the right spot when you add them or drop them here. Every sound you leave alone keeps the normal Dig-n-Build sound.

        To start from the real ones, get the normal sounds as WAV files, edit the ones you want and drop them back in. ADD THE CLASSIC SOUNDS fills every sound you have not changed with the old ones, so you can mix the two.

          USE YOUR PACK

          Packs load from a link. Put your pack file online, then paste the link into the world.

          In the world

          1. Open the options menu and press PACKS.
          2. Pick the TEXTURES, SOUNDS or MUSIC tab, paste your link and press LOAD.
          • Only you see and hear your pack. Everyone else keeps their own.
          • Paste your link again each time you join.
          • Texture packs are a 2048 by 2048 PNG image.
          • Quest loads sound and music packs up to 64 MB. The bar under your pack shows where that is.

          Putting it online

          GitHub Pages works for everyone. Other sites only load for players who turned on Allow Untrusted URLs in their VRChat settings.

          1. Make a GitHub account and a new public repository.
          2. Upload your pack file to it. The website only takes files up to 25 MB, so use GitHub Desktop for bigger ones.
          3. In the repository, open Settings, then Pages, and have it deploy from the main branch.
          4. After a minute your link works. It looks like this:https://yourname.github.io/yourrepo/my-pack.png

          Texture packs can also go on Imgur. Use the direct i.imgur.com link to the image. Imgur turns PNG files over 5 MB into JPG, which breaks glass, leaves and the menus, so keep it under that.
